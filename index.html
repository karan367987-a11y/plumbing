Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What type of roofing work is covered?

This service connects homeowners with contractors for new roof installations and full roof replacements, including tear-offs and storm-related replacements. Repair-only jobs are not handled through this service.

Who can request a callback?

The homeowner or an authorized decision maker for the property. Renters and third parties are not eligible to submit a request.

Is there a charge to submit a request?

No. Submitting a request through this site is free. Any pricing for roofing work is discussed directly with the independent contractor you speak with.

How quickly will I be contacted?

Response times vary by location, contractor availability, and demand. For the fastest service, call (844) 833-0187 directly.

Are the contractors licensed and insured?

Contractors and providers are independent. It is the homeowner's responsibility to verify that any hired contractor holds the required license and insurance for the work being performed.
