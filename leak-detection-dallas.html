What Is Leak Detection?

Leak detection is the process of identifying the exact location of a water leak within a plumbing system. It involves a combination of visual inspection, listening devices, electronic sensors, thermal imaging, and pressure testing to pinpoint where water is escaping from pipes, fittings, or fixtures.

Professional leak detection goes beyond simply noticing a wet spot. It's about determining whether the leak is inside a wall, under a slab, underground, or connected to a fixture. The goal is to locate the source accurately so that repairs can be targeted and efficient, avoiding unnecessary demolition and guesswork.

In Dallas, where many homes have slab-on-grade foundations and extensive underground plumbing, professional leak detection is particularly valuable. Identifying a slab leak or underground line issue early can save thousands of dollars in structural repairs and water bills.

Signs You May Have a Hidden Water Leak

Many leaks remain undetected for weeks or months. Watch for these warning signs.

📈

Unexpected Water Bill Increase

A sudden spike in your water bill without a change in usage is often the first sign of a hidden leak.

👂

Sound of Running Water

Hearing water running when all fixtures are turned off may indicate a leak somewhere in the plumbing system.

🟫

Damp or Discolored Walls

Water stains, peeling paint, or bubbling wallpaper can be signs of moisture behind the surface.

☁️

Ceiling Discoloration

Yellowish or brownish spots on your ceiling often indicate a leak from a pipe above, possibly from a bathroom or roof.

🦠

Mold or Musty Odors

Persistent damp smells or visible mold growth in areas away from obvious moisture sources can point to hidden leaks.

🌡️

Warm or Damp Flooring

Warm spots on tile or concrete floors, especially in slabs, may indicate a hot water line leak beneath the surface.

💧

Low Water Pressure

A sudden or gradual drop in water pressure can be caused by a leak that reduces flow before reaching fixtures.

🌿

Wet Yard Spots

Soggy or unusually green patches in your yard, especially during dry weather, may indicate a leaking underground line.

🔄

Running Water Meter

If your water meter continues to run when all water is off, there is likely a leak somewhere in your system.

Slab Leak Detection Dallas

A slab leak is a water leak that occurs in the pipes located beneath your home's concrete foundation. In the Dallas area, slab-on-grade construction is common, making slab leaks a frequent concern for homeowners. These leaks can be caused by shifting soil, pipe corrosion, improper installation, or high water pressure.

Detecting a slab leak requires specialized equipment because the leak is hidden beneath concrete. Common signs include warm spots on the floor (for hot water lines), unexplained moisture along baseboards, cracks in the foundation, and the sound of running water when all fixtures are off.

Our leak detection Dallas team uses electronic amplification, thermal imaging, and pressure testing to locate slab leaks with precision. Early detection is critical to prevent structural damage and costly foundation repairs.

Leak Detection Technology

Acoustic Leak Detection

Sensitive listening devices amplify the sound of water escaping from pipes, helping to locate leaks in walls, slabs, or underground.

Thermal Imaging

Infrared cameras detect temperature differences caused by moisture, helping to identify hidden leaks without invasive methods.

Moisture Meters

Handheld devices measure moisture content in building materials to confirm the presence and extent of water intrusion.

Electronic Leak Detection

Advanced electronic sensors can detect the exact point of a leak using sound or electrical conductivity, especially useful for slab leaks.

Pressure Testing

By isolating sections of plumbing and monitoring pressure, we can determine if a leak exists and narrow down its location.

Video Pipe Inspection

For drain and sewer lines, small cameras provide visual confirmation of cracks, blockages, or breaks.

Note: Not every method is used on every job. The approach depends on the specific situation and suspected leak type.

What to Do If You Suspect a Water Leak

  1. Check your water meter: Turn off all water and note if the meter continues to run.
  2. Inspect visible areas: Look under sinks, around appliances, and in basements for signs of moisture.
  3. Listen for running water: Quietly walk through your home and listen for the sound of water.
  4. Monitor your water bill: A sudden increase could indicate a hidden leak.
  5. Shut off the water if there's an active leak: Locate your main shut-off valve.
  6. Document any visible damage: Take photos for insurance or reference.
  7. Call a professional: Contact a qualified leak detection specialist for an accurate diagnosis.

Leak Detection vs Leak Repair

While often handled by the same professional, leak detection and leak repair are two distinct processes. Leak detection is the diagnostic phase: identifying if a leak exists, where it is, and what is causing it. Leak repair is the corrective phase: fixing or replacing the damaged pipe or fixture.

Accurate detection is essential because it ensures that repairs address the actual problem. Without proper detection, repairs may be made in the wrong location, leading to repeated issues and unnecessary expense.

Our service can include both detection and repair, or we can provide a detailed diagnostic report for you or another contractor to perform the repair.

Frequently Asked Questions About Leak Detection

What are the signs of a hidden water leak? ▼

Common signs include unexplained water bill increases, sound of running water, damp walls, ceiling stains, musty odors, wet yard spots, and low water pressure. If you notice any of these, it's wise to schedule a professional inspection.

How do plumbers detect hidden water leaks? ▼

Plumbers use specialized equipment like acoustic listening devices, thermal cameras, moisture meters, and pressure testing to locate leaks without damaging property. The method depends on the suspected leak location and type.

How can I tell if I have a slab leak? ▼

Signs of a slab leak include warm spots on floors (for hot water lines), cracks in the foundation, unexplained moisture along baseboards, the sound of running water, and a sudden increase in water bills.

What causes a sudden increase in my water bill? ▼

A sudden spike often indicates a leak. Other causes include a faulty water meter, changes in usage, or an irrigation system problem. A professional can help diagnose the cause.

Can a water leak occur inside a wall? ▼

Yes, leaks inside walls are common. They can be caused by damaged pipes, loose fittings, or condensation. Signs include damp spots, peeling paint, mold, or a musty smell.

How is a leak detected under a concrete slab? ▼

Leaks under slabs are detected using electronic amplification, thermal imaging, or pressure testing to pinpoint the exact location without breaking concrete unnecessarily.

What equipment is used for leak detection? ▼

Common tools include acoustic listening devices, thermal imaging cameras, moisture meters, electronic sensors, and video inspection cameras.

Is professional leak detection non-invasive? ▼

Often yes. Many leaks can be located without demolition using advanced tools. However, some leaks may still require access for confirmation or repair.

How long does leak detection take? ▼

The duration varies depending on the size of the property, complexity of the plumbing, and the type of leak. Most residential inspections take 1-2 hours.

Can a plumber find a leak without breaking the wall? ▼

In many cases, yes. Using thermal imaging, acoustic sensors, and other non-invasive methods, plumbers can locate the leak behind a wall without cutting into it.

What should I do if I have an active water leak? ▼

If safe, turn off the main water supply. Avoid electrical hazards, move valuables away, and contact a professional immediately.

Can low water pressure indicate a leak? ▼

Yes. A leak can reduce water pressure by diverting flow away from fixtures. It can also be caused by a clog or municipal issue.

Why do I hear water running when everything is turned off? ▼

This is a classic sign of a leak. The sound can be from water escaping in walls, under floors, or at a fixture.

Can an underground water line leak? ▼

Yes. Underground water lines can leak due to corrosion, shifting soil, or root intrusion. Signs include wet spots in the yard or sinkholes.

How much does leak detection cost? ▼

Costs vary based on the location, complexity, and method used. Contact us for a personalized estimate after we understand your situation.

Do you provide leak detection in Dallas? ▼

Yes, we serve Dallas and the surrounding metro area. Our team is local and familiar with the area's plumbing systems.
