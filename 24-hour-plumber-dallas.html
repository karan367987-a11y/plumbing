Know the Signs

Common Emergency Situations

Recognize these issues early to avoid costly damage.

Burst Pipe

Sudden flooding, hissing sounds. Turn off main water, call us immediately.

Overflowing Toilet

Water won't stop running. Plunger doesn't help — we clear blockages fast.

No Hot Water

Cold showers, unusual tank noises. Could be heating element or gas valve.

Sewer Backup

Multiple drains clogged, gurgling toilets. Call immediately to prevent contamination.

Gas Leak

Rotten egg smell, hissing. Evacuate, call 911, then call us for repair.

Water Leak Behind Walls

Moldy smell, peeling paint, high bills. We find and fix hidden leaks.
